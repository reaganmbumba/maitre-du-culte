I would like to introduce an application I have prepared for our parishes. It is called Worship Leader, in French, Maître du Culte.

As you know, the worship leader carries a great responsibility. He must follow each part of the service, watch the time, note who speaks, count the attendance, and then write a clean report. And when something does not go as it should, he must know the internal rules of the Church in order to recall them.

This application helps him with all of this, directly on his phone.

During the service, the live worship mode times each part, from the opening prayer to the final blessing. It compares the real time with the planned time and points out when a part runs over.

When there is an irregularity, for example a part that runs too long or a speaker who is absent, the application automatically recalls the article of the internal rules that applies. The worship leader no longer needs to search: the rule is right there, before his eyes. He can also read the full rules, keep his favourite articles, and receive a rule to reflect on every day.

At the end of the service, the report is ready as a PDF, with the attendance, the times and the summary. He can send it in one touch by WhatsApp or by e-mail to the Reverend, to the Apostle, or to the person in charge. All reports are kept in the history.

The application is available in French, Portuguese and English. Each parish receives its own activation, so that everything stays orderly and secure.

Why does this matter? Because the Word tells us, in Ecclesiastes, chapter three, verse one: "To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven." This application helps our parishes respect the time, respect the rules, and report faithfully.
